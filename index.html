Stereo Mix is a hidden Windows feature that captures everything your PC plays — enabling this makes the browser transcribe VLC, YouTube, or any app's audio.

  1. 1
    Right-click the 🔊 speaker icon in the taskbar → click Sound settings
  2. 2
    Scroll down → click More sound settings to open the classic Sound panel
  3. 3
    Go to the Recording tab → right-click empty space → tick Show Disabled Devices
  4. 4
    Right-click Stereo Mix → Enable → then right-click again → Set as Default Device
  5. 5
    Play your VLC video, then come back here, refresh this page (F5), and click Start Listening
✅ After setup: play VLC audio and watch the Level meter above react — that confirms it's working before you start transcribing.

VB-Audio Virtual Cable creates a virtual microphone that captures system audio. More reliable than Stereo Mix and works even if Stereo Mix isn't available on your PC. Download free from vb-audio.com/Cable.

  1. 1
    Download VB-Cable from vb-audio.com/Cable → extract → run VBCABLE_Setup_x64.exe as Administrator → Install Driver
  2. 2
    Restart your PC after installation
  3. 3
    In VLC: go to Audio → Audio Device → select CABLE Input (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)
  4. 4
    Right-click 🔊 in taskbar → Sound settings → More sound settings → Recording tab → right-click CABLE Output → Set as Default Device
  5. 5
    Come back here, refresh (F5), play VLC, watch the Level meter react, then click Start Listening
✅ Tip: VB-Audio lets you continue hearing the audio through your speakers — go to VLC's Audio settings and also enable your normal speaker as a secondary output.
